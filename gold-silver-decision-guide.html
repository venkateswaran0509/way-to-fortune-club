Before you decide

Separate the purpose

Personal jewellery is mainly a consumption choice. Making charges, wastage, stones and resale deductions affect what you recover.

Investment metal is an asset decision. Consider volatility, liquidity, costs, product structure, taxes and concentration in your overall portfolio.

Do not use emergency savings or borrow to speculate. Gold and silver prices can fall sharply; you can lose money.

Understand the wrapper

Common ways to get exposure

Features vary by product and provider. Read current scheme documents, fees, custody terms and tax treatment before acting.

Coins & bars

Consider: direct possession and known physical form.

Costs / risks: dealer premium, storage, insurance, purity checks and resale spread. Silver is bulky relative to value.

Exchange-traded funds

Consider: exchange trading and no personal storage.

Costs / risks: demat/brokerage, expense ratio, tracking difference, market liquidity and fund structure. Read the latest scheme documents.

Mutual funds

Consider: fund access without personally storing metal; structure depends on scheme.

Costs / risks: expense ratio, tracking and redemption terms. Check the scheme mandate, holdings and current documents.

Digital gold: do not assume it has the same protections, oversight, custody or liquidity as a regulated security or mutual fund. Verify the provider, legal structure, custody, redemption terms and risks.

This comparison is general education, not an endorsement. Commodity products can be volatile and may not suit short-term or essential savings.
