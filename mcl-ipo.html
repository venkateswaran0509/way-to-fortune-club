IPO issue size paathuttu, “company-ku indha full amount varum” nu nenakkatheenga. Mahanadi Coalfields IPO 100% Offer for Sale. Coal India than existing shares sell pannudhu; MCL-ku pudhu capital varadhu. Issue amount-um company-oda full market value-um vera.

RHP-padi, FY26-la revenue ₹30,550 crore, profit ₹10,698 crore. FY24-lendhu annual profit rendu varushama kuranjirukku. Aana latest half-year-la, last year same period compare pannumbodhu revenue-um profit-um improve aachu. So long-term trend-um recent update-um rendu paakanum.

Risk side-la, MCL coal business-um power customers-um mela depend aagudhu. RHP-la company-kku against 49 criminal, 111 tax, 52 regulatory proceedings list pannirukkaanga. Quantified amount involved ₹11,813 crore—idhu confirmed loss nu artham illa.

Coal India majority stake-um retain pannum. So, proceeds yaarukku pogudhu, earnings epdi move aagudhu, legal and sector risks enna nu RHP-la check pannunga. Idhu financial education; subscribe or avoid recommendation illa.

On-screen note: “The amount involved in legal proceedings is not the same as an expected or confirmed loss. Read the RHP risk disclosures.” Keep any last-minute IPO dates or subscription figures off the reel unless you recheck them before posting.
